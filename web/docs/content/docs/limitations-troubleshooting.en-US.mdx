## Known limitations

- The beta has no paid SLA or guaranteed service availability.
- Third-party models, websites, stores, MCP servers, and update services may be unavailable by provider, account, network, or region.
- Checkpoint and rollback do not reverse every external side effect.
- Computer Use is sensitive to screen state and is not universal across all apps.
- Office files are not promised as natively parsed documents.
- Running terminal processes may not be fully restored after restart.
- Preview and Experimental features can change or fail more often.

{/* section:diagnostic-order */}
## Safe diagnostic order

1. Record the Lyra version, operating system, affected workspace, and exact time.
2. Reproduce with non-sensitive content and the narrowest feature set.
3. Check local permissions, disk space, network, provider status, credentials, endpoint URL, and rate limits.
4. Disable the relevant third-party Skill, MCP server, or UIUX Pack and retry.
5. Inspect logs after removing secrets; do not publish tokens, prompts, personal files, coordinates, or session cookies.
6. Back up local data before clearing a profile, registry, workspace, or application data.

{/* section:reporting */}
## Reporting a problem

Include concise reproduction steps, expected and observed results, version and platform, and a sanitized log excerpt. Until an official support channel is published and verified on the legal site, do not send sensitive reports to an address merely found in source or third-party pages.
