## Usage essentials

1. Drag and hold any tab to reorder instantly.
2. When tabs overflow, wheel-scroll directly on the tab strip for horizontal navigation.
3. Left controls are back/forward; right `+` creates a new tab.

## Design intent

1. Active tab should visually connect with the content area.
2. Familiar interaction patterns should stay familiar.
3. Keep density high without sacrificing readability and hit targets.
