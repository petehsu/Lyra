LYRA / PRIVACY POLICY

隐私政策 Privacy Policy

我希望这份政策能让你看懂：Lyra 自己知道什么、哪些数据留在本机，以及你主动选择的服务会看到什么。 This policy is meant to make the boundaries clear: what Lyra knows, what stays on your device, and what a service you choose may receive.

生效日期：2026 年 7 月 11 日Effective: July 11, 2026 版本：0.1.0Version: 0.1.0
一句话版本 The short version 账户功能目前只需要 Google 身份信息和少量偏好设置。你的项目、会话和提示词默认留在本机；当你主动选择 AI 提供商或第三方网站时，相关内容会按照你的操作发送给它们。 The account features currently need only Google identity information and a small set of preferences. Your projects, sessions, and prompts stay on your device by default; when you choose an AI provider or third-party website, relevant content is sent according to your action.

1. 范围与联系1. Scope and contact

本政策适用于 Lyra 桌面应用以及链接到本页面的 Lyra 账户服务。Lyra 目前由个人开发者 Pete Hsu 独立维护。如果你有隐私问题、删除请求或数据权利请求，请联系 privacy@lyra.ltd。

This policy applies to the Lyra desktop application and the Lyra account services that link to this page. Lyra is currently operated independently by Pete Hsu. For privacy questions, deletion requests, or data-rights requests, contact privacy@lyra.ltd.

正式发布前请确认运营主体、邮箱、注册地址、适用法域以及是否需要当地代表或数据保护官。页面当前使用的是个人开发者发布初稿。

Before public launch, confirm the operating entity, email address, registered address, governing jurisdiction, and whether a local representative or data protection officer is required. This page is currently a release draft for an independent developer.

2. 我处理什么数据2. Data we handle

Google 账户信息Google account information

当你选择 Google 登录时，Lyra 会从身份服务获得账户 ID、邮箱、显示名称和头像地址，并用它们创建或更新你的 Lyra 账户。Lyra 不会请求或读取 Gmail、Google Drive、联系人、日历或 Google 账户中的其他内容。

When you choose Google sign-in, Lyra receives an account ID, email address, display name, and avatar URL from the identity service to create or update your Lyra account. Lyra does not request or read Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendars, or other Google account content.

账户偏好Account preferences

如果你完成账户引导，Lyra 可能保存语言偏好、主题偏好、引导完成状态和版本号。这些字段存放在 Supabase 的 profiles 表中，并通过行级安全策略限制为你的账户可读写。

If you complete account onboarding, Lyra may save your language preference, theme preference, onboarding state, and onboarding version. These fields are stored in Supabase’s profiles table and protected by row-level security so they are readable and writable only for your account.

Git 邮箱提示Git email hint

启动页可能读取你电脑全局 Git 配置中的邮箱，并向 Lyra 的账户服务发送一个规范化后的邮箱，用于判断它是否对应已有账户。当前实现只返回“存在/不存在”，不会把这个邮箱写入你的 Lyra 资料字段；服务基础设施仍可能按照其日志和安全实践处理请求元数据。

The startup page may read the email in your computer’s global Git configuration and send a normalized email to Lyra’s account service to check whether it matches an existing account. The current implementation returns only whether a match exists and does not write the email to your Lyra profile fields; service infrastructure may still process request metadata under its logging and security practices.

你主动交给 AI 或网站的内容Content you send to AI or websites

提示词、会话、文件、图片、网页内容、浏览器 Cookie、工具参数或其他内容可能在你主动执行相关操作时离开设备。接收方通常是你配置的 AI 提供商、你打开的网站或你授权的本地/远程工具，而不是 Lyra 的 Supabase 账户资料服务。

Prompts, sessions, files, images, web content, browser cookies, tool parameters, or other content may leave your device when you actively perform the relevant action. Recipients are generally the AI provider you configure, the website you open, or a local/remote tool you authorize, not Lyra’s Supabase account-profile service.

3. 我为什么使用这些数据3. Why we use it

  • 完成 Google 登录、恢复会话并保护账户安全；
  • 保存你明确选择的语言、主题和引导状态；
  • 回应支持、隐私、删除和安全请求；
  • 诊断错误、维护服务并防止滥用，但当前没有在这些页面中启用广告追踪或分析脚本。
  • complete Google sign-in, restore sessions, and protect account security;
  • save the language, theme, and onboarding choices you make;
  • respond to support, privacy, deletion, and security requests; and
  • diagnose errors, maintain the service, and prevent abuse. These pages do not currently load advertising or analytics scripts.

适用法律要求时，处理依据可能包括履行你请求的服务、你的同意、履行法律义务以及维护服务安全所需的合法利益。

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing may rely on providing a service you request, your consent, a legal obligation, or legitimate interests in securing and maintaining the service.

4. 我会把数据给谁4. Who receives data

  • Supabase：用于身份验证和账户资料存储。Supabase 作为基础设施服务商处理这些数据。
  • Google：用于完成 OAuth 登录。Google 依据自己的隐私政策处理登录过程。
  • 你选择的 AI 服务：用于生成回答、执行 Agent 操作或处理你主动提交的内容。每个服务的保留和训练政策不同，请单独查看。
  • 你访问的网站和工具：当你使用浏览器、登录管理器或工具调用时，数据会按照你的操作发送给相应的第三方。
  • Supabase: for authentication and account-profile storage as an infrastructure provider.
  • Google: to complete OAuth sign-in. Google processes the sign-in under its own privacy policy.
  • AI services you choose: to generate responses, run agent actions, or process content you actively submit. Retention and training practices vary by service.
  • Websites and tools you access: when you use the browser, login manager, or a tool call, data is sent to the relevant third party according to your action.

我目前没有出售个人信息，也没有为了跨站广告而共享个人信息。若未来改变这一点，我会先更新本政策并提供适用法律要求的选择机制。

I do not currently sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. If that changes, this policy will be updated and any legally required choices will be provided.

5. 本地数据与安全5. Local data and security

Lyra 是桌面软件。项目、会话、设置、浏览器配置和你主动保存的登录管理器数据可能保存在本机。Google 会话 token 由 Electron 的安全存储能力加密保存，renderer 不直接接触 token。

Lyra is a desktop application. Projects, sessions, settings, browser data, and login-manager data you choose to save may remain on your device. Google session tokens are encrypted with Electron’s secure storage facility and are not exposed directly to the renderer.

没有任何软件能保证绝对安全。请使用设备锁、系统更新、可靠的磁盘加密，并不要把不必要的秘密发送给 AI 或网站。

No software can guarantee absolute security. Use a device lock, current system updates, and reliable disk encryption, and do not send unnecessary secrets to an AI service or website.

6. 保留、删除与账户关闭6. Retention, deletion, and account closure

账户资料会在账户存在期间保留，以便登录和同步偏好。你可以通过 privacy@lyra.ltd 请求访问、更正或删除账户资料；我会在核验请求后，按照适用法律处理。

Account-profile data is retained while the account exists so Lyra can provide sign-in and preference synchronization. You may request access, correction, or deletion through privacy@lyra.ltd; after verifying the request, I will handle it as required by applicable law.

删除云端账户资料不会自动删除本机项目、会话、浏览器数据或凭据。请在卸载或删除本地数据前自行备份需要保留的内容。

Deleting cloud account-profile data does not automatically delete local projects, sessions, browser data, or credentials. Back up anything you need before uninstalling Lyra or deleting local data.

Supabase、Google 和你选择的 AI 服务可能按照各自政策保留必要的安全、审计或交易记录。

Supabase, Google, and AI services you choose may retain necessary security, audit, or transaction records under their own policies.

7. 你的选择与隐私权利7. Your choices and rights

根据你的所在地，你可能拥有访问、复制、更正、删除、限制处理、反对处理、数据可携带、撤回同意以及向监管机构投诉的权利。加州居民还可能拥有了解收集类别、请求删除或更正、知悉是否出售或共享，以及拒绝出售或共享的权利；Lyra 当前不出售或共享个人信息。

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, copy, correct, delete, restrict, object, port, withdraw consent, and complain to a supervisory authority. California residents may also have rights to know the categories collected, request deletion or correction, know whether information is sold or shared, and opt out of sale or sharing; Lyra does not currently sell or share personal information.

提交请求时，请使用与账户相关的邮箱，并说明请求范围。为了保护账户，我可能需要验证你的身份；我不会因为你行使隐私权而歧视你。

When submitting a request, use an email associated with the account and describe the scope of the request. I may need to verify your identity to protect the account, and I will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

8. 未成年人8. Children

Lyra 不是面向儿童设计的服务。我不会在明知的情况下收集未达到适用最低年龄的儿童个人信息。如果你认为儿童向 Lyra 提供了个人信息，请联系我，我会调查并在适用时删除。

Lyra is not designed for children. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the minimum age required by applicable law. If you believe a child provided personal information to Lyra, contact me and I will investigate and delete it where required.

9. 跨境处理9. International processing

Supabase、Google 和你选择的 AI 提供商可能在你所在国家或地区以外处理数据。跨境处理的地点、传输机制和保留期限取决于相应服务商的配置和政策；正式发布前我会在服务商清单中补充实际区域。

Supabase, Google, and AI providers you choose may process data outside your country or region. The locations, transfer mechanisms, and retention periods depend on each provider’s configuration and policy; the actual regions will be added to the provider register before public launch.

10. 政策更新10. Changes to this policy

当 Lyra 的数据实践、功能或适用法律发生变化时，我会更新本政策并修改顶部的生效日期。重大变化会尽量通过应用或其他合理方式提示。

I will update this policy when Lyra’s data practices, features, or applicable laws change, and I will update the effective date at the top. Material changes will be announced through the app or another reasonable channel where practical.

For privacy requests, security issues, and deletion requests, contact privacy@lyra.ltd. Confirm that this mailbox exists and is monitored before public launch.
